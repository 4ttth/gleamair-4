1Executive Summary

Gleamair Enterprises operates a well-presented, content-rich corporate website that is materially undermined by three engineering conditions: an unoptimised media payload that makes the site prohibitively expensive to load on Philippine mobile connections, a hand-duplicated front-end that has already begun to diverge across pages, and the complete absence of measurement, meaning commercial performance cannot presently be evidenced. None of these conditions are design faults. All three are correctable within a defined programme of work.

SSECKA IT Solutions was asked to review the Gleamair Enterprises website and to propose implementations that would improve its technical standing and its commercial return. We conducted a full inspection of the production source repository — seven published pages, approximately 6,500 lines of hand-authored HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Python, a JSON-backed content pipeline, and 68 MB of committed media assets.

The review confirms a sound editorial and search-engine foundation. Structured data is correctly implemented, canonical and social metadata are present on every page, all fifty image elements carry alternative text, and the ventilation sizing calculator is a genuine commercial differentiator that few competitors in the sector offer. The decision to hold catalogue content in versioned JSON files rather than hard-coding it into the pages reflects sound instinct and gives the programme a foundation to build upon.

Against that foundation, the measured deficiencies are substantial. The homepage transfers approximately 24.4 MB to a first-time visitor and the products page approximately 21.8 MB; a competitive benchmark for a site of this type is under 2 MB. Not one of the fifty images on the site defers loading. Roughly 1,522 lines of stylesheet are duplicated inline across the seven pages, and that duplication has already produced seven different navigation blocks, five different footers and three divergent brand-token definitions on a site that should have one of each. Three product brochures advertised to prospective buyers return errors because the underlying files were renamed but the links were not. There is no analytics instrumentation of any kind, no automated quality gate, and no route by which a submitted enquiry reaches the Odoo platform the business already operates.

Proposed response

We propose a programme of six core workstreams delivered across three phases over an indicative twelve-week period, with two further optional workstreams available should Gleamair Enterprises wish to extend the engagement into commercial tooling. The phasing is deliberate: Phase 1 delivers the changes that produce immediate, visible improvement at the lowest risk; Phase 2 removes the structural duplication that is the root cause of ongoing maintenance cost; Phase 3 converts the resulting stability into measurable commercial return.

Gleamair Enterprises is a Philippine supplier and installer of ventilation, air conditioning, natural daylighting and solar lighting systems, serving residential, commercial and industrial clients from Olongapo City. Its website performs three commercial functions: it establishes credibility with prospective buyers through project and client evidence, it presents a technical product catalogue with downloadable specification literature, and it captures enquiries through an online contact form, an embedded live-chat channel and marketplace listings.

3Current State Assessment

3.1  Established strengths

It is important to record what is working, because the programme proposed in Section 4 is designed to preserve it. The following were verified during the review and should not be disturbed by any implementation:

  • Search-engine foundation. Organisation and LocalBusiness structured data are correctly expressed as JSON-LD, canonical URLs are declared, Open Graph and Twitter card metadata are complete, and geographic targeting for the Philippine market is set. A sitemap, a robots directive and search-engine ownership verification are all in place.
  • Accessibility fundamentals. All fifty image elements carry alternative text and each page declares exactly one first-level heading. These are frequently absent on comparable sites and their presence here reflects care in authoring.
  • Separation of content from presentation. Catalogue, testimonial, client, project and calculator content is held in versioned JSON files under conf/db/ and applied to the site by a generator script. The instinct is correct and the programme builds on it rather than replacing it.
  • Genuine technical differentiation. The sizing calculator implements documented engineering relationships for building volume, ventilation rate and unit selection across residential, commercial and industrial scenarios. This is a substantive commercial asset.

Root cause

Findings F-02, F-03, F-04 and F-05 share a single cause: the site is maintained entirely by hand, with no build step and no automated verification. This is an entirely reasonable way to have started, and it has carried the site to its present standard. It does not scale to the size the estate has now reached. The programme in Section 4 addresses the cause, not only the symptoms — which is why we recommend Phase 2 be commissioned before further pages are authored.

4Proposed Implementations

The programme comprises six core workstreams and two optional workstreams. Each is specified below with its objective, scope of work, deliverables and indicative effort. Effort is expressed in person-days of senior engineering time and includes analysis, implementation, testing and handover documentation.

Workstream WS-1  ·  Phase 1Performance and Media Remediation

6 days

Objective. Reduce first-visit transfer weight on every page to below 2 MB without any perceptible loss of visual quality, and restore the broken product literature.

Scope of work

  • Re-encode the full image estate to modern formats with quality-matched fallbacks, and generate responsive size variants so that mobile visitors receive appropriately dimensioned files rather than full-resolution originals.
  • Re-encode and rate-limit the homepage hero video, introduce a lightweight poster frame, and defer video loading so that it never blocks the first meaningful paint. Suppress video entirely on constrained connections.
  • Apply deferred loading and explicit dimensions to all fifty image elements, eliminating both the unnecessary fetches identified at F-03 and the layout shift they cause.
  • Restore the three brochure files identified at F-02 and correct the corresponding catalogue references; compress the sixteen megabytes of brochure literature for faster delivery.
  • Establish connection pre-establishment and font preloading for third-party resources, and defer the live-chat widget until after first paint (F-09).

Deliverables

  • Optimised media estate committed to the repository, with originals archived separately.
  • Before-and-after performance report for all seven pages, measured on a simulated Philippine mobile profile.
  • Written media preparation standard for future asset additions.

Workstream WS-2  ·  Phase 2Front-End Consolidation and Build Pipeline

9 days

Objective. Establish a single authoritative definition of every shared element, so that a change is made once and applied everywhere, and eliminate the divergence recorded at F-04.

Scope of work

  • Introduce a static site build using a lightweight template engine, producing the same static output the site produces today and preserving current hosting arrangements without change.
  • Reduce the seven navigation variants, five footer variants and three brand-token definitions to one of each, reconciling the differences with Gleamair Enterprises before adoption.
  • Extract the 1,522 duplicated inline stylesheet lines into a shared, cached stylesheet with a single brand-token layer, retaining page-specific styles where genuinely page-specific.
  • Separate behaviour from markup by replacing the fourteen inline event handlers, enabling a content security policy to be introduced under WS-6.
  • Verify rendered output against the current site page by page to confirm no visual regression.

Deliverables

  • Build pipeline producing byte-comparable static output, with a documented local development workflow.
  • Consolidated layout templates, shared stylesheet and single brand-token definition.
  • Visual regression comparison covering all seven pages.

Workstream WS-3  ·  Phase 2Content Management and Data Integrity

7 days

Objective. Enable Gleamair Enterprises staff to publish catalogue, project and testimonial content without developer involvement, and prevent malformed content from reaching production (F-05).

Scope of work

  • Introduce a Git-backed editorial interface over the existing conf/db/ content files, giving non-technical staff a browser-based editor while retaining full version history and requiring no additional hosting or database.
  • Define and enforce a formal schema for each content file, so that a missing image, an invalid product record or a malformed specification is rejected at edit time rather than discovered by a visitor.
  • Automate the generation step so that publishing no longer depends on a script being run on a developer workstation.
  • Extend the content model to capture the fields currently absent from the catalogue, including per-product literature references, so that F-02 cannot recur.

Deliverables

  • Editorial interface configured for catalogue, projects, testimonials, clients and mission content.
  • Content schemas with validation integrated into the delivery pipeline.
  • Illustrated editor guide and a training session for nominated staff.

Workstream WS-4  ·  Phase 3Enquiry Capture and Odoo Integration

5 days

Objective. Ensure every enquiry is captured as a structured, tracked record in the Odoo platform Gleamair Enterprises already operates, and that every enquirer receives immediate acknowledgement (F-06).

Scope of work

  • Route contact form submissions into Odoo CRM as qualified leads, carrying the product interest, project type and enquiry source, with the existing electronic mail notification retained as a fallback.
  • Issue an automatic branded acknowledgement to the enquirer stating expected response time.
  • Add server-side validation and proportionate automated-submission protection ahead of the relay.
  • Extend the sizing calculator so that a completed calculation may be submitted as a specification enquiry, carrying the computed requirement and recommended model into the resulting lead record.
  • Reconcile the live-chat channel and the contact form so that both originate leads in the same place.

Deliverables

  • Enquiry integration in operation, with fallback behaviour tested against a simulated outage.
  • Calculator-to-enquiry submission path.
  • Lead-source taxonomy documented for reporting under WS-5.

Workstream WS-5  ·  Phases 1 and 3Measurement, Analytics and Search Optimisation

4 days

Objective. Make commercial performance visible, so that the effect of this programme and of all future marketing expenditure can be evidenced (F-08, F-10).

Scope of work

  • Implement privacy-respecting analytics across all pages, with a baseline established in Phase 1 before remediation begins so that improvement can be demonstrated against it.
  • Define and instrument the commercial events that matter: enquiry submission, calculator completion, brochure download, live-chat initiation and marketplace referral.
  • Register and configure search-engine webmaster properties, and extend the sitemap with last-modified signals generated automatically at build time.
  • Extend structured data to individual products, making catalogue items eligible for enhanced search presentation.
  • Deliver a monthly performance report covering traffic, source, page performance and enquiry conversion.

Deliverables

  • Analytics property with commercial event tracking and a documented measurement plan.
  • Search-engine properties configured and verified.
  • Reporting template with the first month’s report delivered.

Workstream WS-6  ·  Phases 1 and 3Quality Assurance and Delivery Automation

5 days

Objective. Prevent the recurrence of the defect class represented by F-02 by verifying every change automatically before it reaches visitors (F-07).

Scope of work

  • Establish a continuous integration pipeline that, on every change, validates content files against their schemas, verifies that every internal link and asset reference resolves, and builds the site.
  • Enforce a page-weight budget so that any change taking a page above the agreed threshold fails the pipeline rather than reaching visitors.
  • Introduce automated performance and accessibility scoring with agreed minimum thresholds.
  • Automate deployment on successful validation, with a documented one-step rollback.
  • Introduce a content security policy and appropriate response headers, enabled by the separation of behaviour from markup delivered under WS-2.

Deliverables

  • Pipeline configuration committed to the repository, with all checks passing.
  • Documented deployment and rollback procedure.
  • Security header and policy configuration.

4.2  Basis of estimate

Effort has been estimated from the bottom up against the measured scope recorded in Appendix A, rather than from a percentage of a notional project value. Each workstream was decomposed into its constituent tasks and each task sized against the quantity of work actually present: seven pages to consolidate, approximately 1,522 lines of duplicated stylesheet to extract, fifty image elements to correct, an estate of sixty-eight megabytes of media to re-encode, fourteen products across four categories to model, and nine brochures to verify and compress. Where a task’s size depends on a fact we could not establish by static inspection — principally the configuration of the client’s Odoo tenancy — that dependency is stated in Section 8.1 and the associated risk recorded at R-03.

A person-day is a full working day of senior engineering time and is inclusive of analysis, implementation, testing, review and the documentation required at handover. It is not a measure of coding time alone. Project management, weekly reporting and the phase review meetings described in Section 5.1 are included within the stated effort and are not charged separately.

The estimates carry no separate contingency line. Each phase is offered on a fixed-price basis, which means SSECKA IT Solutions carries the risk of the estimate being wrong for the scope stated. What the fixed price does not absorb is a change to that scope, which is handled under the procedure at Section 8.4. Should any workstream complete in materially less effort than estimated, the saving is reflected in the final invoice for that phase rather than retained.

8Assumptions, Dependencies and Exclusions

The effort and schedule stated in this proposal rest on the following assumptions. Should any prove incorrect, SSECKA IT Solutions will notify Gleamair Enterprises in writing and state the effect before any additional effort is incurred.

  • The repository inspected during this review is the authoritative source of the production site, and no undisclosed changes are applied directly to the published site outside it.
  • Original, full-resolution source files are available for the product photography and the homepage video, or the committed versions are of sufficient quality to serve as originals.
  • Gleamair Enterprises holds, or can obtain, administrative access to its Odoo tenancy, domain registration, hosting arrangement and marketplace accounts as required by the relevant workstream.
  • The business holds the necessary rights to all photography, client logos and brochure literature published on the site.
  • A nominated client representative is available for review and decision within three (3) working days of a request.
  • The site remains a static publication. No workstream in this proposal requires an application server or a database.

  • Repository access with permission to create branches and open changes for review.
